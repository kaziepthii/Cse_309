# Problem Statement

## The Problem
Many people struggle to manage their personal finances effectively. They often:
- Don't know where their money is going
- Forget to track their daily expenses
- Have difficulty identifying spending patterns
- Cannot easily edit or correct mistakes in their records
- Lack a simple tool to track income and expenses

## Existing Solutions
Currently, people use:
- Spreadsheets (Excel/Google Sheets) - Complex and time-consuming
- Pen and paper - Not organized, hard to maintain
- Mobile apps - Often require subscriptions or have limited features

## Why Our Solution?
Our Personal Finance Tracker provides:
- A simple and intuitive interface
- Full CRUD operations (Create, Read, Update, Delete)
- Real-time balance calculation
- Secure user authentication
- Easy transaction management

## Impact
- Users can track their finances easily
- Users can identify spending patterns
- Users can make better financial decisions
- Users can correct mistakes quickly with the update feature
